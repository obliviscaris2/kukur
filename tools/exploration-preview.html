KUKUR · EXPLORATION UPDATE

Little wonders, under your paws.

Worn paving, soft earth, climbing gardens and junkiris. These are captures from the running game.

Play KUKUR ↗

Space hop and climb · E make a wish or rest · F bark to stir petals · Q sniff for discoveries. Use M to find Ratna Park, Patan or Bhaktapur.

The dog has a fuller silhouette, planted paws and gentler spine, head and tail follow-through. Stones, logs and benches have matching solid surfaces. This expands exploration around the valley; it does not make every decorative object interactive.

Performance is still below a dependable 30 FPS on the Intel HD 4600. Recent 1280×720 busy-street samples measured 13–17 FPS; the later run used 163 draws and 533k triangles. Distant trees use simpler shading and fewer overlapping leaves; particle effects add no shadow lights. Validation and performance notes · Painted asset sources
